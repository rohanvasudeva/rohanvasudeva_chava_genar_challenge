**Periodic Adverse Drug Experience Report (PADER) Draft**

**Executive Summary**

This report summarizes the adverse drug experience data for Bisoprolol, as reported during the period from December 27, 2024, to December 26, 2025. The dataset contains 1024 unique cases, with 1023 reported as serious and 68 reported as fatal. The most frequently reported reactions include Acute kidney injury, Drug ineffective, and Hypotension.

**Reporting Period**

The reporting period for this analysis is from December 27, 2024, to December 26, 2025.

**Case Overview**

A total of 1024 unique cases were reported during the analysis period, with 1023 (99.9%) of these cases reported as serious and 1 (0.1%) reported as non-serious. Additionally, 1023 (99.9%) cases were reported as expedited.

**Seriousness Analysis**

Of the 1024 total cases, 1023 (99.9%) were reported as serious. The seriousness criteria for these cases include death (67), life-threatening (105), hospitalization (480), disabling (43), congenital anomaly (7), and other (905).

**Expedited Case Analysis**

All 1023 serious cases were also reported as expedited.

**Demographic Analysis**

The age distribution of the reported cases is as follows: 76+ years (379, 37.01%), 61-75 years (362, 35.35%), 46-60 years (134, 13.09%), Unknown (87, 8.5%), 31-45 years (36, 3.52%), <18 years (16, 1.56%), and 18-30 years (10, 0.98%). The sex distribution is: female (503, 49.12%), male (493, 48.14%), and Unknown (28, 2.73%). The country distribution is led by EU (325, 31.74%), United Kingdom (278, 27.15%), and France (187, 18.26%).

**Adverse Reaction Analysis**

The most frequently reported reactions in the overall dataset include Acute kidney injury (80), Drug ineffective (54), Hypotension (46), Drug interaction (43), and Dyspnoea (38).

**Serious Reaction Analysis**

The serious reaction dataset is similar to the overall reaction dataset, with the top reported reactions being Acute kidney injury (80), Drug ineffective (53), Hypotension (46), Drug interaction (43), and Dyspnoea (38).

**Fatal Case Analysis**

In the 68 fatal cases, the most frequently reported reactions include Acute kidney injury (7), Death (6), Respiratory failure (6), Fall (5), and Pneumonitis (4).

**Outcome Analysis**

The outcome of the reported cases is as follows: Recovered/Resolved (494, 48.24%), Unknown (330, 32.23%), Recovering/Resolving (227, 22.17%), Not recovered/Not resolved/Ongoing (195, 19.04%), Fatal (68, 6.64%), and Recovered/Resolved with sequelae (21, 2.05%).

**Geographic Distribution**

The country distribution of the reported cases is led by EU (325, 31.74%), United Kingdom (278, 27.15%), and France (187, 18.26%).

**Temporal Trends**

The monthly case reports show a varying trend, with the highest number of cases reported in July 2025 (109) and the lowest in December 2024 (21).

**Limitations**

This report is based on the supplied evidence packet and does not include any additional information or analysis. The available dataset does not support conclusions regarding the causality of the reported adverse events.

**Conclusion**

This PADER draft summarizes the adverse drug experience data for Bisoprolol during the specified reporting period. The report highlights the most frequently reported reactions, demographic characteristics, and outcomes of the cases. However, it is essential to note that the reported adverse events may not be causally related to Bisoprolol, and further analysis is required to determine the relationship between the drug and the reported reactions.
